\noindent\textbf{Problem 2.} Give a model showing that two formulas are not logically equivalent.\\[0.75em]

\noindent\textbf{Student work}\\[0.4em]
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
Domain: & $\{1,2\}$ \\
Constants: & $a = 1$ \\
Predicates: & $P = \{1\}$
\end{tabular}

\vspace{1.1em}
\noindent\textbf{Claim checked}\\[0.4em]
$\forall x\,Px$ \hspace{1em} and \hspace{1em} $\exists x\,Px$

\vspace{1.1em}
\noindent Since only $1$ is in $P$, the universal sentence is false but the existential sentence is true.
