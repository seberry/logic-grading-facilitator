Quick reference

Symbols

  • ¬ (negation) — shortcuts: ~ or -
  • ∧ (conjunction) — shortcuts: & or .
  • ∨ (disjunction) — shortcuts: v or V
  • → (conditional) — shortcuts: -> or =>
  • ↔ (biconditional) — shortcut: <-> or =
  • ⊥ (contradiction) — shortcuts: _|_ or F

Justifications

  • Pr — premise (auto-counted when blank/Pr at the top; type Pr for an explicit extra premise).
  • Hyp — subproof assumption (auto-added to the first line of any subproof if left blank).
  • Rule + cites, e.g., ∨E 2,4 or →I 2-5, for standard rules.
